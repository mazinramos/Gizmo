<h2>Overview</h2>



<p>

Gizmo Customer is the customer-facing part of the Gizmo ecosystem, designed to provide users with a simple and accessible shopping experience.

</p>



<h2>Purpose</h2>



<p>

The application focuses on the customer side of the platform and provides the interface through which customers interact with the store.

</p>



<h2>Main Areas</h2>



<ul>

<li>Customer shopping experience</li>

<li>Product browsing</li>

<li>Customer interactions</li>
